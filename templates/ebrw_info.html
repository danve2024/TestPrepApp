EBRW Practice Test

Welcome

Welcome to the Examlet full-length English section practice test. This comprehensive assessment is designed to help you prepare for the SAT Reading and Writing section by providing a realistic testing experience.

The test is timed, but you can pause it at any time if you need a break. When you finish the practice, you'll be able to see your detailed score and review all your answers on the Progress page. Use of a calculator during this part is permitted, though it's typically not needed for Reading and Writing questions.

Good luck! You've got this! 🎯

Test Structure

Two Parts

Each part is 32 minutes long with no breaks between them.

27 Questions Per Part

Each part contains 27 carefully selected questions covering various skills and difficulty levels.

Pause Anytime

You can pause and resume the timer whenever you need to take a break.

Question Types

Vocabulary (Questions 1-5)

Test your knowledge of word meanings and usage.

Words in Context & Function (Questions 6-7)

Understand how words function within passages and sentences.

Reading Comprehension (Questions 8-15)

Analyze passages and answer questions about their content and structure.

Standard English Conventions (Questions 16-22)

Demonstrate your understanding of grammar, punctuation, and sentence structure.

Transitions & Synthesis (Questions 23-26)

Work with transitions and synthesize information from multiple sources.
